Order Cancellation Feature

Enhanced Customer Control for LPG Gas Booking System

Key Features

Customer Protection

Customers can cancel orders until a delivery person accepts them, providing flexibility while maintaining fairness.

Delivery Staff Protection

Once a delivery person accepts an order, it cannot be cancelled, ensuring they get compensated for their commitment.

Security & Validation

Multiple security checks ensure customers can only cancel their own orders, with comprehensive validation.

Real-time Updates

Dynamic UI updates show cancellation eligibility in real-time, preventing confusion and errors.

Cancellation Policy

When You Can Cancel

  • Pending Orders: Orders that haven't been confirmed by admin yet
  • Confirmed Orders: Orders confirmed by admin but no delivery person assigned
  • Time Limit: You can cancel until a delivery person accepts your order
  • Security: You can only cancel your own orders for security reasons

How It Works

Place Order

Customer places LPG cylinder order

Admin Confirms

Admin reviews and confirms the order

Cancellation Window

Customer can cancel during this period

Delivery Accepted

Delivery person accepts - no more cancellation
