About LIAA LPG

Your trusted partner for safe, reliable, and efficient LPG gas delivery services across Kerala.

Our Story

Since our establishment in 2020, LIAA LPG has been dedicated to providing premium LPG gas delivery services to households and businesses across Kerala. We understand the importance of reliable energy supply in your daily life and business operations.

With a commitment to safety, quality, and customer satisfaction, we have built a reputation as a trusted LPG service provider. Our team of experienced professionals ensures that every cylinder delivery meets the highest safety standards.

We continuously invest in modern technology and training to improve our services and provide you with the best possible experience. From easy online booking to real-time tracking, we make gas delivery simple and convenient.
