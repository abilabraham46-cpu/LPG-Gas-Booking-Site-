Real Payment Setup

Configure your LPG delivery system to receive real payments in your Google Pay account

What You Get

  • Customers pay directly to your Google Pay UPI ID
  • Real money transfers (not demo/test payments)
  • Manual payment verification for security
  • Admin panel to approve/reject transactions
  • Automatic order status updates after verification
  • Support for all UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
  • Complete transaction history
  • Mobile-responsive payment interface

Setup Instructions

Step 1

Get Your Google Pay UPI ID

First, you need to find your Google Pay UPI ID:

  1. Open Google Pay app on your phone
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right)
  3. Tap "Bank account"
  4. Your UPI ID will be shown (like: yourname@okaxis, yourname@ybl)
  5. Note it down - you'll need this for Step 2
Important: Make sure this UPI ID is active and can receive payments.
Step 2

Update Payment Configuration

Edit the Google Pay payment file with your real UPI ID:

// File: payments/gpay_payment.php // Line 24-25: Replace with your actual details $merchant_upi_id = "your-actual-upi@okaxis"; // YOUR REAL UPI ID HERE $merchant_name = "Your Business Name"; // YOUR BUSINESS NAME

Example:

$merchant_upi_id = "johnsmith@paytm"; $merchant_name = "John's LPG Delivery Service";
Step 3

Test the Payment System

Test the complete flow:

  1. Make a test order in the customer portal
  2. Click "Pay Now" and select "Google Pay"
  3. It will open Google Pay with your UPI ID as recipient
  4. Complete a small test payment (like ₹1)
  5. Submit the transaction ID on the payment page
  6. Go to admin panel to verify the payment
Step 4

Verify Payments (Admin)

Use the admin panel to verify real payments:

  • Access: admin/payment-verification.php
  • Check your Google Pay for incoming payments
  • Match transaction IDs with payments received
  • Click "Verify" to approve or "Reject" to decline
  • Orders automatically update when verified

How It Works

1. Customer Orders

Customer places LPG order

2. Google Pay

Customer pays to your UPI ID

3. You Verify

Admin verifies payment received

4. Auto Delivery

Order processes automatically
